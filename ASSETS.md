It is not an original vector master supplied by the company. `Brand.tsx` displays the FO emblem in an SVG viewport; the masthead and welcome wordmarks use scalable HTML typography to keep small lettering crisp. The original `brand-logo.jpg` is retained as the source reference.

Image 1 is the original identity reference. Image 2 is the transparent cutout to correct. Edit image 2 only: clean away every white, cyan, blue and red fringing artifact outside the true logo edges, and remove the exaggerated bright outlines around the letters. Restore the restrained original colors and original smooth silver lettering from image 1, especially the dark red ON box; do not add bevels, extra strokes, glow, shadows, or saturation. Preserve the same FO emblem, same stacked layout, same letter shapes and exact wording FRAME ON PRODUCTIONS. Keep the brushed blue-steel FO and the warm highlight from the original image. Output the complete clean professional logo on genuine transparent alpha, including transparent holes inside all letters. No background, no square card, no checkerboard. Leave modest transparent padding and keep every letter intact.
